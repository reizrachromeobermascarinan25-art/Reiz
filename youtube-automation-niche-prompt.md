# AI YouTube Automation — Untapped Monetizable Niche Prompts

## How to Use This File
Copy any prompt below into an AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) to generate scripts, titles, thumbnails ideas, and content plans for faceless YouTube channels in niches that are **low competition but high monetization potential**.

## 1. Micro-History of Everyday Objects
**Niche:** Short documentaries (8-12 min) on the surprisingly deep history behind mundane items — zippers, doorknobs, traffic cones, rubber bands.

**Why it works:** History content gets high CPM ($8-15), very few channels cover *object-level* history, and it's endlessly scalable.

**Prompt:**
```
You are a YouTube scriptwriter specializing in fascinating micro-histories.
Write a compelling 10-minute video script about the hidden history of [OBJECT].

Structure:
- Hook (first 15 seconds): Start with a shocking or counterintuitive fact
- Origin story: Where and when was it invented? What problem did it solve?
- Unexpected twists: Wars, controversies, or bizarre moments in its history
- Modern impact: How it shaped the world in ways nobody realizes
- Closing CTA: Tease the next episode and ask viewers to guess the next object

Tone: Conversational, witty, Neil deGrasse Tyson meets Vox. Use vivid imagery
that works well with stock footage and AI-generated visuals.
Include timestamps and visual direction notes in [brackets].
```

## 2. AI-Narrated "What If" Geography
**Niche:** Hypothetical geography scenarios — "What if the Sahara flooded?", "What if Antarctica had no ice?", maps and terrain analysis.

**Why it works:** Map/geography content is booming but "what if" geography is barely tapped. High watch time, great for AI visuals, CPM $6-12.

**Prompt:**
```
You are a geographic analyst and engaging storyteller for a YouTube channel
called "Alternate Atlas." Write a 12-minute script for a video titled:
"What If [GEOGRAPHIC SCENARIO]?"

Structure:
- Cold open with a dramatic visualization description of the scenario
- Scientific breakdown: What would physically happen step by step?
- Human impact: How would civilizations, economies, and migration change?
- Ecological consequences: Wildlife, climate shifts, new biomes
- Timeline: Map out changes at 1 year, 10 years, 100 years, 1000 years
- Comparison to real-world parallels

Include notes for [MAP ANIMATIONS], [SATELLITE IMAGERY], and [AI-GENERATED
TERRAIN VISUALS] throughout. Tone: authoritative but accessible.
End with a poll question for the audience.
```

## 3. "Decoded" — Hidden Math & Science in Daily Life
**Niche:** Revealing the hidden algorithms, physics, and math behind things people interact with daily — elevator logic, Netflix recommendations, grocery store layouts.

**Why it works:** Education + mystery = high retention. CPM $10-18 (education/tech niche). Very few channels do "invisible systems" content.

**Prompt:**
```
You are a science communicator for a faceless YouTube channel called "Decoded."
Write a script (8-10 min) revealing the hidden science behind [EVERYDAY SYSTEM].

Structure:
- Hook: "You interact with this every day and have no idea how insanely
  complex it really is..."
- Surface level: What people THINK is happening
- The reveal: The actual math/science/engineering underneath
- Deep dive: Walk through the algorithm or physics step by step with
  simple analogies
- Mind-blow moment: One fact that reframes how the viewer sees it forever
- "Now you'll never look at [THING] the same way again" closing

Use analogies a 15-year-old would understand. Include [ANIMATION NOTES]
for motion graphics. Suggest 3 thumbnail concepts with text overlay ideas.
```

## 4. Abandoned & Forgotten Megaprojects
**Niche:** Deep dives into massive engineering projects that were started but never finished — cancelled cities, half-built bridges, abandoned space programs.

**Why it works:** Urbex/abandoned content is huge, but *megaproject* storytelling is a gap. High CPM ($7-14), strong binge potential.

**Prompt:**
```
Write a 12-minute YouTube script for a faceless documentary channel about
an abandoned megaproject: [PROJECT NAME].

Structure:
- Teaser: Show what it was SUPPOSED to look like (describe AI visual)
- The ambitious vision: Who planned it and why? What was the dream?
- The rise: Early progress, funding, public excitement
- The turning point: What went wrong? Politics, money, engineering failure?
- The aftermath: What remains today? Can it be revived?
- Legacy: How it influenced future projects

Tone: cinematic, slightly melancholic, like a great documentary narrator.
Include [B-ROLL SUGGESTIONS] using stock footage keywords and
[AI IMAGE PROMPTS] for recreating the original vision.
Suggest SEO-optimized title and 5 tags.
```

## 5. "Price of" — Economics Behind Luxury & Rare Items
**Niche:** Breaking down why extremely expensive or rare things cost what they do — a $50K tuna, a $2M parking spot, the world's most expensive spice.

**Why it works:** Finance/economics CPM is $12-25. "Why is X so expensive" is proven but very few channels own this as a *brand*. Endless content supply.

**Prompt:**
```
You are an economics storyteller for a YouTube channel called "Price Of."
Write a 10-minute script explaining: "Why Does [ITEM] Cost [PRICE]?"

Structure:
- Hook: State the absurd price. Show a visual comparison
  ("That's more than a house in [CITY]")
- Supply chain breakdown: Raw materials, labor, scarcity, logistics
- The hidden costs nobody talks about (regulation, insurance, expertise)
- Who actually buys this and why?
- Price over time: Has it always been this expensive?
- The "is it worth it?" verdict with a surprising angle

Tone: Curious, slightly incredulous, data-driven but entertaining.
Include specific numbers and statistics throughout.
Add [INFOGRAPHIC NOTES] and [COMPARISON VISUAL] directions.
Generate 3 title options (curiosity-gap style) and thumbnail text ideas.
```

## 6. Silent Disasters — Slow Crises Nobody Covers
**Niche:** Long-form content about ongoing global crises that don't make headlines — aquifer depletion, soil degradation, light pollution effects, sand shortages.

**Why it works:** Documentary CPM is high ($8-16). These topics have almost zero YouTube competition but massive search potential as awareness grows.

**Prompt:**
```
Write a 15-minute documentary-style YouTube script about a slow-moving
crisis: [CRISIS TOPIC].

Structure:
- Opening: A single powerful image or scenario that makes it personal
  ("Imagine turning on your tap and nothing comes out")
- What is actually happening? Explain the crisis clearly
- Scale: Numbers, maps, affected regions
- Why nobody is talking about it (media incentives, slow timelines)
- Who IS affected right now? Real human stories (research-based)
- What happens if nothing changes? 10-year and 50-year projections
- Solutions that exist but aren't being implemented
- What viewers can actually do (keep it realistic, not preachy)

Tone: Urgent but calm. Think Kurzgesagt meets Vice.
Include [DATA VISUALIZATION] notes and [MAP OVERLAY] directions.
End with a question that drives comments.
```

## 7. "One Rule Changed" — Sports & Games Alternate Histories
**Niche:** Exploring how sports, board games, or video games would be completely different if one rule had been changed.

**Why it works:** Sports + gaming CPM $6-14. "What if" content drives debates and comments (algorithm fuel). Barely explored niche.

**Prompt:**
```
Write an entertaining 10-minute YouTube script for a channel called
"One Rule Changed." Topic: "What If [SPORT/GAME] Changed [RULE]?"

Structure:
- Hook: State the rule change and immediately show one wild consequence
- Current rule explained: Why does it exist? Brief history
- The butterfly effect: Walk through cascading changes step by step
  - Strategy changes
  - Which historical champions would have won/lost
  - How the fan experience changes
  - Economic and cultural ripple effects
- Expert-style analysis with specific examples and stats
- Community poll: "Would you prefer this version?"

Tone: Sports commentator energy meets thought experiment.
Include [GRAPHIC/ANIMATION NOTES] for visual breakdowns.
Generate 5 SEO tags and a curiosity-gap title.
```

### Key Tips for AI YouTube Automation
1. **Use AI voiceover** (ElevenLabs, Play.ht) — pick a unique voice and keep it consistent
2. **AI visuals** (Midjourney, DALL-E, Runway) for thumbnails and B-roll
3. **Post 3-4x per week** minimum in the first 3 months
4. **Optimize for watch time** — scripts should have hooks every 2-3 minutes
5. **Build series playlists** — binge-watching signals boost the algorithm
6. **Diversify revenue early** — AdSense + sponsorships + affiliate links + merch
